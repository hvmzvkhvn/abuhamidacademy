WELCOME TO ABU HAMID ACADEMY

Rooted in knowledge.
Dedicated to community.

Abu Hamid Academy serves the Muslim community of Leeds and the surrounding areas through Islamic education grounded in the Sunni scholarly tradition.

Our teaching brings together Qur’an, tajweed, and an Islamic studies curriculum, with a commitment to nurturing understanding, character, and a meaningful connection to faith.
