ABOUT THE ACADEMY

A local commitment.
A lasting foundation.

Founded by Ustad Akeel Jeraal, Abu Hamid Academy responds to the educational needs of the Muslim community in Leeds and the surrounding areas. The Academy offers a welcoming setting in which children, teenagers, and adults can develop their knowledge of Islam.

Our programmes encompass Qur’an, tajweed, and an Islamic studies curriculum. Adult learners can pursue a one year diploma in Fard Ayn, focusing on the religious knowledge needed for personal practice.

Through attentive teaching and engagement with the local community, the Academy seeks to cultivate informed faith, sound character, and a sense of responsibility towards others.
