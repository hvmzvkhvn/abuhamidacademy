Born and raised in Leeds, Ustad Akeel Jeraal completed the Micro Madrasa programme in 2015 under the guidance of Shaykh Ibrahim Osi Efa and Shaykh Haroon Hanif. He subsequently pursued Arabic studies through the Arabic Madrasa programme under Shaykh Haroon Hanif. Following its completion, he continued his Islamic studies with Shaykh Abdul Hafeez at Bradford Muslim College.

Ustad Akeel remains committed to further study with teachers in Istanbul, including Dr Hamza Bekri, Dr Salah al-Shami, Dr Ahmad Snobar, Dr Bassem Itani, and Shaykh Abd al-Hamid al-Turkmani. His continuing studies reflect an enduring commitment to learning within the Sunni scholarly tradition.

Alongside his full time work as an IT consultant, Ustad Akeel teaches at institutes in Bradford and supports local organisations. His community engagement has a particular focus on young people and grassroots work in Leeds.

This experience inspired him to establish Abu Hamid Academy to address the educational needs of the Muslim community in Leeds and the surrounding areas. Through the Academy, he seeks to make Islamic learning accessible across generations and to support the development of knowledge, character, and community.
